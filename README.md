# Titanic: Last Lifeboat

A single-player browser survival game. **Play it here: https://oughton.github.io/titanic-survival/**

April 14th, 1912. The Titanic has struck an iceberg and is sinking by the bow. There are not enough lifeboats for the 60 passengers: you and 59 bots. How long can you survive?

## Phase 1: the sinking ship
- Move with **WASD** or the arrow keys. Hold **Shift** to sprint, which also lets you shove through crowds.
- Lifeboats open for boarding at different times, and each has only 2–4 seats. Watch the map at the top of the screen.
- Stand at a loading boat's gangway and **hold Space** to board. Only the person at the front of the gangway can board.
- The water rises from the bow and freezes you quickly. Stay on the dry part of the ship, near the stern.
- Deck chairs, crates and pianos slide down the tilting deck, and funnels collapse.

## Phase 2: the lifeboat
- Steer with **← →**. Row hard with **↑** or **Space**, and slow down with **↓**.
- Any iceberg will sink you. Sharks with **red-edged dark fins** will capsize you; grey-finned sharks are harmless.
- Survive until the **Carpathia** arrives at 04:10.

Passengers are ranked by how long they survive. **P** pauses the game and **M** mutes the sound. Touch screens are supported.

Plain HTML5 canvas and JavaScript, with no build step. To play locally, open `index.html`.
