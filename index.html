TITANICLAST LIFEBOAT

April 14th, 1912 — 23:40. The ship has struck an iceberg and is sinking by the bow.
There are not enough lifeboats for everyone. How long can you survive?

1 · On the ship

  • Move with WASD / arrows, Shift to sprint and shove.
  • Lifeboats open at different times. Watch the map at the top.
  • Stand at a loading gangway and hold Space to board. Only the person at the front can board, so push through the crowd.
  • The water rises from the bow. It freezes you fast, so keep to the dry stern.
  • Watch out for sliding deck chairs and pianos, and for collapsing funnels.

2 · In the lifeboat

  • Steer with ← →. Row hard with ↑ or Space, slow down with ↓.
  • Hitting any iceberg sinks your boat.
  • Sharks with red-edged dark fins are man-eaters and will capsize you. Grey fins are harmless.
  • Hold on until the Carpathia arrives at 04:10.
  • The earlier you board, the longer you must survive at sea.

You and 59 other passengers. P pause · M mute · on touch screens use the left stick and the right button.
